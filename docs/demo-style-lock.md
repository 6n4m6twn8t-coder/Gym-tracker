# Final demo style lock

Leg Press is the benchmark for the finished movement-demo system.

- Dark, fixed-camera render.
- Same athlete, machine, camera, lighting and framing across every frame.
- Controlled 3-second rep loop.
- Full-foot contact with the plate.
- Natural hip, knee and ankle mechanics.
- No morphing, camera movement or detached joints.
- Quads may be subtly highlighted, but the movement must remain the priority.
- The anatomy card remains a separate system and is not changed by demo work.

Approved direction: polished rendered athlete/machine style rather than schematic/mannequin SVG art.
